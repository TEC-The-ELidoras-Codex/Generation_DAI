# CRAP test

Use this quick checklist when evaluating a viral short:

- Currency: How new is it? Is it a re-upload?
- Reliability: Do physics/biology make sense? Any visual seams?
- Authority: Who posted it? Is there a credible source?
- Purpose: Inform, entertain, or farm clicks/emotion?

It’s OK to enjoy it—but label it correctly in your mind.
